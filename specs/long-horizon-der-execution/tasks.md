- T1 and T3 can proceed in parallel; T4 depends on the stable state contract from T2/T3.
- T5–T10 depend on the ledger and persistence schema and must not become independent guard patches. T8–T10 may proceed in parallel with T5–T7 after the lifecycle schema is agreed.
- T11–T12 can proceed in parallel with T5–T10 after the final provenance contract is agreed.
- T13–T17 require the contracts and implementation waves to land first and must share fixtures with the behavioral loop.
- T18 is the acceptance gate. Long-horizon readiness is not declared until search → synthesis → render, restart/resume, persistence outage, and a multi-domain task complete without repeated resolver loops or physics veto of required work.
- The phase math/scheduler implementation is **NO CHANGE (verified)** for this feature; its boundary receives contract tests rather than new semantic coupling.
- T19–T21 (REQ-11/12/13) can proceed in parallel with each other; T22 (the encoder-absent acceptance replay) depends on all three landing, since it exercises scoring, synthesis, and fold-forward together.
- T23–T24 (REQ-14) can proceed independently of T25–T26 (REQ-15); both build on the ALREADY-EXISTING `useTaskProgress.ts` merge-by-id and send-guard-removal behavior (NO CHANGE) rather than depending on any Wave 1–4 work.
- T27–T29 (REQ-16) must land together: intercepting `open_url` (T27) without removing the duplicate implementation (T28) or fixing the capability gate (T29) leaves a partial fix. T30 (REQ-17) can proceed in parallel — it pins the existing seam contract and does not depend on T27–T29.
- T31 (REQ-18) depends on T19, T20, T23, T25/T26, and T27 each landing far enough to emit the fields it traces; it can be developed against stubs before all six are complete.
- T32–T34 are the Wave 5–8 acceptance gate. **T32 is the top-level acceptance gate for this entire extension**: a websearch task completing end to end with the encoder absent (evidence → synthesis → one Prism card → no re-gather loop) must be demonstrated before `specs/phase-4-encoder` REQ-8 measures the encoder as an improvement over this baseline.
- T35–T36 (REQ-19) have no code dependency on any prior wave — they are documentation/docstring/test additions and can proceed at any time; land them early since T19–T44 SHOULD use the canonical names once the table exists.
- T37–T39 (REQ-20) are independent of T1–T34; T39 is blocked on the Open Question's answer (migrate/discard/leave), not on any other task.

Doing T37/T38 first converts every downstream requirement from unfalsifiable to testable; doing them last means every earlier wave is validated against a database the application does not actually use.
- T40–T42 (REQ-21) depend on REQ-2's ledger (T2) and REQ-9's lifecycle persistence (T8) existing, since the footprint's `coordinate_ref` and resume behavior lean on both; T40 (dataclass field) can land before T41 (population logic).
