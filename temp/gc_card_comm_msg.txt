fix(goal-contract): card-settle rides the grade; ceiling skips error envelopes

Two distinct defects proven live (backend-20260912-103123.log, conv-105):

1. Card stayed 'Active Execution' forever while the grade read 'capped
   (failure-settled)'. _der_report_run_grade is the single deduped terminal
   authority — called on failure-settled, finalize-complete, and
   continuation-done — but the card's settle event only fired at the end
   of _execute_plan_der. Grade ran, card never settled, so the frontend's
   anyCardWorking gate routed the next message into a dead steer.

2. A VERIFIED step whose result was an envelope error (ws, 'not
   recognized') got ceiling facts mined from the error string itself
   (probe 3: 'wc : The term 'wc' is not recognized' became a discovered
   fact). `_gc_result_is_error` now rejects envelope errors at ceiling time.

Fix: `_der_emit_card_settle` (called from `_der_report_run_grade`'s choke,
agreeing with the existing dedup key) emits `task:done`/`task:fail` by card
identity and conversation so the card always settles exactly when the grade
fires. Outcome is derived from the grade text.

Tests: backend/tests/contract/test_card_settle_contract.py (CT-CS1..4),
4 passed. Combined with the permission contract + approval-class suite 29
passed after fixing the earlier wording test (its label assertion captured
the old spelling; the rendered copy was already correct).
