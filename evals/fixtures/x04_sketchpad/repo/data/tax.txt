SHIPPING TAX RULES 2026

Tax is added to the shipping cost of an order and depends on the region of its zone.
For zones in the north region the tax rate is 5 percent.
For zones in the south region the tax rate is 8 percent.
For zones in the east region the tax rate is 6 percent.
For zones in the west region the tax rate is 10 percent.
