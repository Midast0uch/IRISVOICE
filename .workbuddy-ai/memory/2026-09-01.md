# 2026-09-01 (session 280)

## Wake word "Hey Iris" — root-caused and fixed

**Root cause: `DEFAULT_THRESHOLD` was 0.80, but the wake phrase's own peak
score is 0.773–0.786.** The threshold sat above the signal it was meant to
catch, so detection was mathematically impossible — no amount of pipeline
fixing could have made it fire.

### How the bad 0.80 got in
Session 278 raised 0.65 → 0.80 because "silence scores up to 0.598". That
0.598 is the **mel-buffer startup transient**: openWakeWord and the violawake
SDK both seed `melspectrogram_buffer` with `np.ones((76,32))`, which is far out
of distribution. It decays in ~1 s. Steady-state digital silence is **0.401**.
A transient was measured, mistaken for steady state, and used to set a
threshold above the true positive.

### Hypotheses refuted (do not re-chase)
- **Backbone SHA-256 mismatch** (s279's lead): installed hashes combine to
  `e8444299a314fbb2...` == `MODEL_REGISTRY["oww_backbone"].sha256`. Exact match.
  There is also no `<model>.config.json` (the SDK looks for `.config.json`,
  not `.json`), so no per-model pin exists either.
- **SDK streaming mel ≠ openWakeWord**: measured **bit-exact**
  (`max|diff|=0.000000`, `cos=1.000000`) on silence/noise/chirp/real wav.
- **Ring-buffer wrap after 10 s**: probes at t=5/20/40 s scored identically
  (0.7885 / 0.7862 / 0.7861).
- **512→320 buffering**, **head outputs ~0.5 for everything**: both refuted.

### The measurement that unblocked it
`data/hey_iris_synthesized_test.wav` is **24000 Hz** and was fed to a 16000 Hz
detector. Fed naively: 0.594 (== silence). Resampled to 16 kHz: **0.784**.
The earlier "synthetic speech doesn't trigger" conclusion was invalid.

Measured peaks (real detector, 512-sample engine frames):
`Hey Iris`@16k **0.773–0.786** | quiet room tone 0.572–0.599 |
loud broadband 0.420 | speech-like 0.376 | tonal/music 0.303 |
steady-state silence 0.401.

### Changes
- `backend/voice/violawake_detector.py`: `DEFAULT_THRESHOLD` 0.8 → **0.70**
  (floor; `_threshold = max(0.70, sensitivity)`, sensitivity default 0.65).
  Clears worst negative by 0.10, sits 0.07 under weakest positive.
- `backend/audio/engine.py` + same detector file: removed the s279 temporary
  `rms diag` / `score diag` logs.
