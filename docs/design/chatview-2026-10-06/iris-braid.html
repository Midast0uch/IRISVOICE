IRIS Braid and Spine

IRIS remembers in a way no other harness does. It keeps a time line of what happened, proven landmarks, and a graph of what led to what. The interface should let you see that memory at work, and the Xur should be the body of the agent, not a loading spinner.

Scroll to the working wing to try it. Everything below is a concept. Nothing in the app has changed yet.

01The Xur is the agent

The logo curve becomes the agent's body. It sits in the prompt and listens. When IRIS works, it moves into the matrix, to the row that runs. Its shape follows the work: wide loops when IRIS explores, a tight curve when it narrows down.

02The spine

A thin line runs down every chat. Each turn is a bead on it. When IRIS recalls something, a filament reaches back to the place it came from, even in another chat. A proven fact leaves a star.

03The braid

One thread holds many chats, like strands of one braid: build, plan, a peer, a swarm. All strands share one memory. The braid sits on the wing's top edge, so a switch is one click.

04@ and #

@ picks who hears you. In a peer chat, only @iris sends a prompt. # picks what to remember: a task card, an artifact, a landmark. IRIS gets the address, and its memory does the rest.

05The lens

Click an artifact and it opens inside the wing. Drag it to the dashboard workspace, or pop it out to its own window. The whole wing can detach too.

Try it

Switch strands on the braid. In build, press Run the turn and follow the Xur. In ana, type a message with and without @iris. Type # to add a reference. Click the artifact in plan, or drag it to the dashboard. The ⌖ button opens the map of the whole thread.

Why the thread list lags today

Each time you open the list, the app downloads every thread with every message in it. It then rebuilds the whole conversation state and animates about 780 rows. Picture a librarian who carries every book to the desk each time you ask for the catalogue.

Proof: openHistory() calls fetchConversations() on each open (chat-view.tsx:2928). That fetch maps the full messages of every thread and replaces conversations. Each row is a motion.div. The fix: the list reads only titles and counts, it is virtualized, and messages load only for the strand you open.
