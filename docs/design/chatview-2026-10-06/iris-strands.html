IRIS Living Spine

The Xur pours its trail down the chat as the spine. When IRIS works, the spine gathers back into the Xur at the step that runs, in both modes. Above it, the wing's top edge is one quiet line. The spotlight aperture sits in that line, and a slow light leaves it when work happens.

Change the brand colour above the wing. The Xur takes three neighbouring hues from it. The first concept stays as it was, for reference.

  • Strands open from ⌖The ⌖ icon is back in the header. It opens the map of the strands. The current strand shows under the thread name, with no dropdown arrow. Add a strand in the map, with preset tags or your own.
  • The edge lightThe header trails are gone. The top edge of each wing is one hairline, with the aperture set into it at the centre. At rest, a faint light leaves the aperture every few seconds. When a strand works, the light takes that strand's colour and comes more often. In spotlight, the light stays on near the aperture.
  • The spine runs through the cardIn personal mode, the spine bends into the task card and becomes its step line. The Xur rides it to the step that runs, as in developer mode.
  • DiffsAn edit shows a small ± icon where it happens. Click it to review the change, keep it or undo it. Drag it to the dashboard workspace, like an artifact.
  • Folder and file tabsBoth modes have the project bar above the message box: the folder, the open files, and + to add a folder or repo.
  • Spotlight as todayEach wing has its aperture at the top middle, on the edge. The other wing shrinks to 360 px, fades and blurs, and takes no clicks, as the app does now. Pinned threads keep their place on the Xur's lower loops.

Try it

Press Run the turn in each mode. IRIS asks before it runs the tests: press y or click Allow. While it works, type in the message box to steer it, or press the stop button. Click a ± icon to review a diff, or drag it to the dashboard. Click ⌖ to switch strands. Click the Xur for your threads. Click the small diamond on the top edge of a wing for spotlight.

What I checked for each mode

You were right about the diff review. So I went through what a person does in each mode, and asked what the interface must give them. In this mock means you can try it above. Next means it is part of the build plan.

Developer mode

  • IN THIS MOCKReview diffs. A ± icon at each edit, one review for the whole turn, keep or undo per change, drag to the dashboard.
  • IN THIS MOCKApprove commands in the turn. The row waits for y / n and leaves a receipt.
  • IN THIS MOCKSteer or stop while IRIS works. Type to steer (the steering inbox exists today); the stop button ends the turn and says so.
  • IN THIS MOCKKnow where IRIS works. Folder, open files and branch above the message box.
  • NEXTShell output that stays. A >cmd is one EXEC row whose output opens in place.
  • NEXTUndo a whole turn. "Go back to before this turn" restores the files and the chat (revert exists for the chat today).
  • NEXTKeys. Ctrl K threads, Ctrl J strands, Ctrl D diff review, Esc stop.

Personal mode

  • NEXTVoice as today. The wake word and the voice path stay; the header Xur shows when IRIS listens.
  • IN THIS MOCKSee the work without the jargon. The vertical card keeps every event in plain words, and the spine runs through it.
  • IN THIS MOCKKnow when another strand finishes. A dot on ⌖ while it works, the edge light in its colour, and a short note when it is done.
  • IN THIS MOCKSources under research answers. One quiet row of source chips, not a card (see “budget rules”).
  • NEXTSpoken word follows the text. The word highlight that exists today, in the new reply face.
  • NEXTFiles in, files out. Drop a file on the message box to attach it; artifacts open in the lens.

Both modes, and the network

  • IN THIS MOCKWho hears this. The to: chip. With people, only @iris sends a prompt.
  • IN THIS MOCKHelpers get their own strand and report back to the strand that started them, as a knot.
  • NEXTWho is here. In a strand with people: who is online, who is typing, and a signed mark on each message (Nostr).
  • NEXTFind anything. One search over threads, strands and turns, from the thread orbit.
  • NEXTQuiet motion. With reduced motion on, the trails stand still and only the running step shows change.
