Real-time global jet stream visualization

JETSTREAM is a live, interactive map of the world's jet streams. It animates global upper-level winds, their speed, direction and intensity, at the 200, 250 and 300 hPa pressure levels where the polar-front and subtropical jet streams flow. Wind data comes from the NOAA GFS model via Open-Meteo and is rendered as a flowing particle field over a dark, draggable 3D globe or a flat world map. Drag to rotate, scroll or pinch to zoom, switch pressure levels, and open the timeline to view the last 5 days of history or the 7-day jet stream forecast.
